The **Usage** page shows accepted public submissions for the current workspace during the current UTC calendar month. Every workspace member can view it.

{/* docs-check: production-setting billing.quota-enforcement */}

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Period | The first through the last day of the current month, in UTC |
| Submissions received | Public submissions accepted during the period, across every form in the workspace |
| Included submissions | The monthly submissions included in the workspace plan |
| Grace allowance | Extra submissions the plan allows after the workspace uses its included submissions |

The **Monthly allowance** progress bar compares submissions received with the included submissions plus the grace allowance. A status label shows how much of the included allowance the workspace has used.

## What counts as a submission

Formbrew records usage when it stores an accepted submission. These requests do not count:

- Submissions rejected by validation.
- Requests from origins that are not allowed.
- Submissions that fill in the honeypot input.
- Requests rejected by rate limits.
- Requests to deactivated or archived forms.

Changing a submission's status to **Spam** or **Archived** does not reduce usage. A new period starts on the first day of each month, in UTC.

{/* docs-check: production-setting ai-spam-check */}

Spam that the [AI spam check](/dashboard-guide/ai-spam-check) catches doesn't count. If you return it to the inbox with **Not spam**, it counts then.

## Plans

Your workspace plan sets the included submissions, grace allowance, active webhook endpoints per form, and team seats.

## Subscriptions

The **Subscriptions** page shows the workspace's current plan. Only the workspace owner and members with the **Manage billing** permission can open it. See [Members and roles](/dashboard-guide/members).

{/* docs-check: production-setting billing.purchases */}

Only the workspace owner can subscribe:

1. Open **Subscriptions** and choose Indie, Pro, or Scale. Each is a monthly workspace subscription.
2. Select **Subscribe to** the plan, then pay in Stripe Checkout.
3. Back in Formbrew, the page shows **Confirming your subscription** until Stripe confirms it. The Free plan stays active until then.

Once the subscription is active, the owner can select **Manage subscription** to manage the plan, payment details, and invoices in Stripe's billing portal.

## Current limitations

Historical usage charts are not available.
