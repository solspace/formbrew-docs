---
title: "Manage webhooks"
description: "Create webhook destinations and monitor submission delivery attempts."
---

Webhooks send a signed `submission.created` event to your server after Formbrew accepts a submission. Open a form and select **Webhooks** to manage destinations and inspect recent deliveries.

Owners, admins, and editors can manage webhook endpoints. Viewers can see endpoints and deliveries.

## Create an endpoint

Select **Add endpoint** and provide:

| Setting | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Endpoint name | 1 to 100 characters, for example `CRM intake` |
| Destination URL | An HTTPS URL of at most 2,048 characters |

Select **Create endpoint**.

Destinations must use HTTPS on port 443 and resolve to public network addresses. Formbrew does not follow redirects. Your endpoint must respond within 10 seconds.

## Active endpoint limit

Your workspace plan sets how many active endpoints each form can have. The **Webhook endpoints** section shows how many are active and the limit. Disabled endpoints do not count toward the limit.

When the form reaches the limit, **Add endpoint** is unavailable. Disable or delete an endpoint before you add or enable another one.

If a form has more enabled endpoints than the plan allows, only the oldest ones receive events. The others show **Plan paused**.

## Store the signing secret

Formbrew displays the complete `whsec_...` signing secret once, after you create an endpoint or rotate its secret. Select **Copy secret** and store it in your server-side secret manager before you close the dialog.

<Warning>
  You cannot view the complete signing secret again. Do not put it in frontend code. Rotate it if it may have been exposed.
</Warning>

## Edit, disable, rotate, and delete

Each endpoint card shows whether the endpoint is **Live**, **Paused**, or **Plan paused**, and the last characters of its secret.

- Select **Edit** to change the name or URL, or to clear **Endpoint enabled**. Disabling an endpoint stops new deliveries and cancels queued retries.
- Changing the destination URL cancels queued retries.
- Select **Rotate secret**, then **Rotate and reveal**, to replace the secret. The old secret stops working immediately, and Formbrew cancels queued retries.
- Select **Delete**, then **Delete endpoint**, to remove the endpoint. Deleting cancels queued retries and frees endpoint capacity.

A delivery already in progress may still finish after a configuration change.

## Recent deliveries

The **Recent deliveries** table shows the latest 20 delivery records across the form. Select **Refresh** to reload it.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Waiting for its first attempt |
| Delivering | Formbrew is sending the request |
| Retry scheduled | The last attempt failed, and Formbrew scheduled another |
| Succeeded | The destination returned a `2xx` response |
| Abandoned | Formbrew makes no more attempts |
| Canceled | An endpoint configuration change canceled the delivery |

Formbrew retries network errors, timeouts, and `408`, `425`, `429`, and `5xx` responses. Other responses, including redirects and most `4xx` responses, abandon the delivery immediately.

Formbrew attempts delivery immediately, then after approximately 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, and 2 hours, for at most five attempts. When your endpoint returns a `Retry-After` header, Formbrew waits at least that long, up to 24 hours. The dashboard does not currently support manual retries or test events.

Continue with [Receive webhooks](/webhooks/overview) to implement your endpoint and [Verify webhook signatures](/webhooks/verify-signatures) before trusting payloads.
