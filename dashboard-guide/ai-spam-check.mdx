The AI spam check reads each new submission and moves spam to **Spam** before Formbrew sends any notification, autoresponder, or webhook for it. Submitters never wait: Formbrew accepts the submission at once and checks it afterward.

The AI spam check is available on the Indie, Pro, and Scale plans. It is off until an owner or admin turns it on.

## Turn on the AI spam check

1. Open **Settings** for the workspace.
2. Under **AI spam check**, read how submissions are processed.
3. Select the acceptance checkbox, then select **Turn on the AI spam check**.

Formbrew records who accepted the notice and when. Agents and management tokens can't turn the check on or off. Select **Turn off** to stop checking new submissions; submissions already being checked finish normally.

### What is sent for checking

When the check is on, Formbrew sends each new submission to an AI model run by Anthropic, one of Formbrew's [subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors). The model only classifies the submission as spam or not spam. Formbrew sends:

- The form name.
- Every answer with its field label and type, including hidden fields.
- The submitter's browser user agent.

Formbrew doesn't send request metadata, origins, IDs, or workspace details. Long answers are shortened. Anthropic doesn't use the content to train models and keeps it for up to 30 days.

### Opt a form out

Every form in the workspace is checked unless it opts out. Open the form, select the form settings, and clear **Check this form's submissions** under **AI spam check**. The change applies to new submissions at once. Owners, admins, and editors can change it.

## While a submission is checked

A checked submission shows **Pending checks** for a few seconds, and at most about two minutes:

- Its owner notification, autoresponder, and webhooks wait.
- You can open it, but you can't change its status or notes or move it to trash.
- It doesn't appear in Management API lists or exports unless you ask for it with `includePending=true`.

If the check can't finish in time, Formbrew releases the submission as a normal submission and sends its deliveries.

## When a submission is spam

Formbrew moves it to **Spam** with a reason: promotional, scam, malicious links, gibberish, abusive, prompt injection, or other. Its notifications, autoresponder, and webhooks aren't sent, and it doesn't count toward your monthly submissions.

If a real message was marked as spam, select **Not spam** in the **Spam** view. Formbrew returns it to the inbox, counts it toward your usage, and sends its owner notification and webhooks. The submitter doesn't receive a late autoresponder. The same happens when an agent changes the status of a submission the AI marked as spam.

Submissions you mark as spam yourself still count toward usage. Spam moves to Trash 30 days after it was marked, whoever marked it. See [Spam](/dashboard-guide/submissions#spam).

## Monthly checks

Each plan includes as many AI checks per month as its submission hard limit: 2,200 on Indie, 11,000 on Pro, and 55,000 on Scale. A check that fails or times out doesn't use one.

When a workspace uses all of its checks, the **Spam** view and each form's settings show **AI spam checks paused until** the first day of the next month. Until then, new submissions are accepted, delivered, and counted without a check.

## In the Management API and MCP

Submission responses include:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `processingState` | `pending` while checks run, otherwise `complete`. |
| `aiSpamCheck` | `state` (`pending`, `completed`, `failed`, or `timed_out`), `verdict` (`spam` or `not_spam`), and `category`. `null` when the submission wasn't checked. |
| `spamMarkedAt` | When the submission was marked as spam. |

Changing the status, notes, or trash state of a pending submission returns `409` with `SUBMISSION_PENDING_CHECKS`; try again shortly. Webhook deliveries held back for spam have the `SUPPRESSED` status. Webhook payloads and emails never include check results.

## Current limitations

{/* docs-check: production-setting billing.quota-enforcement */}

- Spam counts toward usage until its verdict arrives, a few seconds after acceptance. During a spam wave, a workspace at its hard limit can briefly reject real submissions with `402` and `PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED`.
- You can't add your own instructions, allow trusted senders, or check older submissions again.
