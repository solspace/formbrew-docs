title: "Members and roles"
description: "Invite teammates, manage roles and permissions, transfer ownership, and leave workspaces."
---

Each workspace has one owner. Other members have a role that controls what they can do. Owners and admins manage members in the **Members** section of workspace settings.

To open workspace settings, open the menu with your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Then select the **Settings for** item that shows the workspace name.

## Roles

| Role | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Has full access, including ownership transfer and workspace deletion. Each workspace has exactly one owner. |
| Admin | Manages members, workspace settings, and every form. |
| Editor | Builds forms and works with submissions. |
| Viewer | Reads forms and submissions. |

| Action | Owner | Admin | Editor | Viewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View forms, submissions, webhooks, and usage | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create, edit, publish, and archive forms | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Update submission status and notes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| View and manage public form tokens | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage webhook endpoints | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Export submissions | Yes | Yes | Yes | With permission |
| Open **Subscriptions** | Yes | With permission | With permission | With permission |
| Change workspace name and slug | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Invite, change, and remove members | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Transfer ownership, delete, and restore the workspace | Yes | No | No | No |
| Leave the workspace | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

The dashboard does not hide every editing control from viewers. If a viewer tries to save a change, Formbrew rejects the change.

## Additional permissions

You can give any member these permissions on top of their role:

- **Export submissions** lets a viewer download submissions as JSON.
- **Manage billing** lets a member open the **Subscriptions** page. Admins do not have this permission by default.

## Seats

Every active member, including the owner, uses one seat. Each pending invitation reserves a seat too. The **Members** section shows how many seats are active or reserved, and the workspace's limit.

<Warning>
  New workspaces start on the Free plan, which includes one seat. The owner uses that seat, so you cannot invite members to a Free workspace. Plans with more seats are not available for purchase yet.
</Warning>

When a workspace has no free seat, inviting a member fails with **Every team seat is in use**.

## Invite a member

1. Open workspace settings.
2. Under **Invite a member**, enter the person's **Email**.
3. Choose a **Role**. The default is **Editor**.
4. Optionally select additional permissions.
5. Select **Send invitation**.

Formbrew emails an invitation link to that address. The invitation expires after seven days.

Pending invitations appear under **Pending invitations** with their expiry date.

- Select **Resend** to send a new link and restart the seven days. The previous link stops working.
- Select **Revoke** to cancel the invitation and free its seat.

Neither action asks for confirmation. After an invitation expires, invite the person again.

## Accept an invitation

Open the invitation link and sign in with the account that uses the invited email address. You must have a verified email address. Select **Accept invitation** to join, or **Decline** to reject the invitation.

If you are already signed in with a matching email address, pending invitations also appear in the workspace switcher and on the dashboard home page. Select **Accept** or **Decline** there.

Accepting an invitation opens the workspace's **Forms** page. It counts toward your [workspace membership limit](/dashboard-guide/workspaces#workspace-membership-limit).

<Note>
  If you sign in with a different email address, Formbrew rejects the invitation. Log out, then open the invitation link again and sign in with the invited address.
</Note>

## Change a member's role or permissions

In the **Members** section, change the member's **Role** or additional permissions. Then select **Save member**. You cannot change the owner's role.

## Remove a member

Select **Remove** next to the member, then select **Remove member** to confirm. The member loses access immediately. An owner or admin must invite them again before they can return. You cannot remove the owner.

## Suspended members

If a workspace's seat limit decreases, Formbrew suspends the most recently joined members until the active members fit. It keeps the owner active.

Suspended members keep their role and permissions, but they cannot open the workspace's data. They can still leave the workspace. An owner or admin can select **Reactivate** when a seat is free.

## Leave a workspace

Members other than the owner can leave a workspace.

1. Open the menu with your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Select the **Leave** item that shows the workspace name.
3. Select **Leave workspace**.

You lose access immediately, without a confirmation step. An owner or admin must invite you again to restore access. Leaving frees one of your workspace memberships.

To leave a workspace you own, transfer ownership first.

## Transfer ownership

Only the owner can transfer ownership. Open workspace settings and use **Transfer ownership**.

1. Under **New owner**, choose **Active member** and select a member, or choose **External verified email** and enter an email address.
2. Under **Your access after transfer**, choose **Remain Admin**, **Remain Editor**, or **Leave workspace**.
3. Select **Transfer now** for a member, or **Send ownership transfer** for an email address.

<Warning>
  **Transfer now** takes effect immediately and has no confirmation step.
</Warning>

A transfer to an email address sends a link that expires after seven days. The recipient must sign in with that verified email address and select **Accept ownership**. The address cannot belong to a current member. While the transfer is pending, you can select **Resend** or **Cancel transfer**. A workspace can have only one pending transfer.

If you want to stay in the workspace after an email transfer, the workspace needs a free seat for the new owner. Otherwise, **Leave workspace** is the only option.
